# Final submission requirements

Checked against the three course PDFs supplied by the owner on 8 November 2026. The Final Report and Self-Reflection guidelines are dated 31 October; the Presentation guideline is dated 5 November. The owner's working completion target is 15 November. These notes summarise the supplied documents; the group should also follow its actual Moodle submission fields and assigned presentation slot.

## Final report

- A self-contained academic or technical report in portrait layout. Approximately 10–20 pages is normally appropriate; no strict page limit. Submit PDF or Word.
- Required: an approximately 250-word abstract with an explicit research gap, a team contribution table, references and a Declaration of generative AI use even when empty.
- Explain the completed technical work and evidence in the report itself. Source code, scripts and executable prototypes must not be submitted for marking. Useful implementation screenshots may be included with explanations.
- Compare and connect related work and develop the review beyond the plan. Attribute methods, data, borrowed visuals and ideas where used.
- Incomplete planned tasks are permitted if completed and proposed work are distinguished. Expected, illustrative or synthetic outcomes cannot be reported as observed results.
- The AI declaration identifies the tool and model/version, affected material and exact prompts used for incorporated material. For a long conversation, provide its purpose, stages and use plus those exact prompts. A full transcript is not required. Exploration with no incorporated material need not be declared.
- The contribution table must name all five members and describe actual completed work. The owner has deferred drafting this table; it remains a required final-submission item.

| Report criterion | Weight within report |
| --- | ---: |
| Technicality and depth | 30% |
| Critical thinking and interpretation | 20% |
| Abstract and research gap | 15% |
| Literature and background | 15% |
| Structure and writing | 10% |
| References | 5% |
| Contribution table | 5% |

Sources: Final Report guideline pages 1–4 for requirements and pages 5–8 for the rubric. The report is 45% of the project mark.

## Presentation

- Ten minutes, no Q&A. All five members speak; equal time is not mandatory.
- Each slide identifies its current speaker by full name. Select an accessible technical example or diagram rather than trying to recite every report detail.
- Submit one group slide set as PowerPoint or exported PDF by 16 November 2026. The actual talk must use the submitted substantive content. Late slide submission causes the specified 50% reduction in presentation marks.
- Rubric: content and clarity 30%, structure and time management 20%, slides and visual communication 20% (shared), delivery 30% (individual). The presentation is 20% of the project mark.

Source: Final Presentation guideline pages 1–4. This does not establish the group's assigned speaking date.

## Individual self reflection

- Each member separately submits approximately 1–2 pages, identifying the student and project group, as PDF or Word.
- Discuss actual personal contributions, specific learning, a challenge or decision, and what to do differently. It is a reflection on experience, not a summary of the group report.
- AI declaration is not required for this particular deliverable. Personal experiences and contributions must nevertheless be real.
- Rubric: reflection and learning 70%, specificity and clarity 30%. This is 10% of the project mark.

Source: Individual Self-Reflection guideline pages 1–2.
